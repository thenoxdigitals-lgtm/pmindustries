Heat Shrinkable  Busbar Insulation Tubing (Red)
Our range of tubing is available in red and brown colors and valued for its superior performance parameters. Widely used for providing outstanding interconnectivity, the collection of Heat Shrinkable Busbar Insulation Tubing is most suitable for underground power. Further, our quality auditors inspect the tubing on many quality parameters.
Busbars insulated are corona discharge free even with shorter spacing between phase to phase. This enables compact switchgear panel design. It offers good protection from environmental effects, ultra-violet radiation, and moisture. The tough cross-linked polyolefin material provides good mechanical protection.
