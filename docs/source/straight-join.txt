Heat Shrinkable Straight Joint
PERFORMANCE CERTIFICATE
"PM-Seal" terminations & joints are satisfactorily engaged to provide fast and easy installation with reliable performance.
       the terminations & joints designed to compliance the type test or exceed the requirements of applicable national and international cable and cable accessories standards like IS:13573(2011) IEC:60502-4 IEEE:48-404 CENELEC HD 629.1/VDE:0278.
DESCRIPTIONS
The termination & joint kits are heat shrinkable designed for extruded' armored and unarmored cables. The design has been focused to provide the basic important functions required for medium voltage power cable accessories including the electrical performance stress relief measures, environmental seal,
        mechanical fatigue & other related performance parameters laid down in different countries for various service conditions.
        The installed terminations virtually duplicate a cable systems performance electrically thermally and mechanically.
        The design is flexible to accommodate out of round or off tolerance cable in both round & sector shaped conductors.
SALIENT FEATURES
Fits a wide dimensional range
Accommodation of tolerances, different conductors cross section & cable constructions
Repeatable results
Excellent weather & UV resistance
Exceptional insulation properties
High tracking resistance in harsh environment
Shelf life limitations are unlimited time for below or up to 50°C
